PinchPlan

A seated, hands-first mixed-reality focus desk. Pinch, pull and pin your tasks, then squeeze into focus with the timer orb.

How to use (hands only – no controllers needed)
  • Pinch a card quickly → moves it to the next lane.
  • Pinch & drag a card → move it between Next up / Now / Done.
  • Drag a card below the board → delete it.
  • Pinch the orb → start / pause a focus session (5, 15 or 25 min).
  • Turn your left palm up → wrist menu (add task, clear done, recenter).
Works seated in a two-foot radius. Everything is saved on this device.
